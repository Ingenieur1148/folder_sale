Portal Quotations

Allow your customers to view and manage their quotations directly from the portal

Compatible with Odoo 18

Features

  • ✅ View quotations list from the portal
  • ✅ Download quotation as PDF
  • ✅ Print quotation
  • ✅ Communication history (chatter)
  • ✅ Secure access — customers see only their own quotations
  • ✅ Compatible with Odoo 18

How it works

Once installed, your portal customers will see a new My Quotations entry on their portal home page. They can browse their quotations, view details, download PDFs and communicate directly with your team.
